- **Request Confirmation** (`req_cnf`) - Optional binding key
- **Expiration times** (`expires_in`, `expires_on`) - Not PII

**Note:** The ID token itself contains PII (username, name, subject, etc.) encoded within it, but the `jsonDictionary` doesn't extract/expose individual claims—it just passes the entire token as-is.
