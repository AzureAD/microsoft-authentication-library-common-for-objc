**Finding 1 — 🟡 Concern: Self-referential `expires_in` test assertion**
- **File:** `IdentityCore/tests/MSIDBrowserNativeMessageGetTokenResponseTests.m`
- **Line:** 438
- **Action:** fix
- **Source:** review-fresh-eyes (HIGH confidence)
- **Details:** `testJsonDictionary_whenCachedPathWithSanitizationFlightEnabled` builds `expectedJson[@"expires_in"]` from `response.jsonDictionary[@"expires_in"]`, then asserts `expectedJson == response.jsonDictionary`. This is circular — `expires_in` validates against itself. Worse, `jsonDictionary` is called twice and `expires_in` is computed from `timeIntervalSinceNow`, so the two calls can produce different values on slow CI, causing flaky failures. Fix: assert individual fields and use `isKindOfClass:NSString.class` for `expires_in` (matching the pattern at line 398).
